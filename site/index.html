Powered by NEAR Intents

Hold it anywhere. Pay on any chain.

When no single balance covers a payment, Ink combines them. Each part goes to NEAR Intents, which swaps and carries it, then pays out the exact amount on the chain the payee asked for.

Not only invoices

Say it the way you'd say it.

Everyday sends, swaps and staking go through the same loop: you ask, the agent proposes a plan, you review and sign.

  • Send 2 NEAR to alice.near
  • Swap 25 USDC to NEAR
  • Stake 10 NEAR
  • Deposit 10 USDC on Base
  • What's my wallet worth?
  • Pay this invoice

Nothing moves until you sign.

  • The agent can't sign

    It reads and proposes. It has no tool that signs, approves or touches keys.

  • Keys stay on your computer

    Encrypted with your password. No Ottopus server and no custody.

  • You review the real thing

    The review window decodes what will be signed, not the agent's words.

  • Open source

    Read every line on GitHub.

cd ottopus-ink
npm ci
npm start
